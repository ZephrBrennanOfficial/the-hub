# Quill — LinkedIn Ghostwriting for Founders

> You know you should post on LinkedIn. You don't. I write your posts for you.

## What This Is

A ghostwriting service for founders and business owners who want an active LinkedIn presence but don't have the time to write.

**Your voice. Your opinions. Your posts.** I just do the writing.

## How It Works

1. **45-min call** — I learn your voice, opinions, and tone
2. **First 4 posts free** — I write them, you review them. If you don't like them, you walk away
3. **You post** — You publish on your schedule
4. **Ongoing** — 15 posts/month. You review, approve, post. 15 minutes of your time per week

## What You Get

- 8–20 LinkedIn posts per month (tier-dependent), written in your voice
- 45-minute onboarding call to capture your tone and perspective
- Posts delivered in a shared Google Doc — review, tweak, approve
- Engagement tracking — I watch what works and adjust
- Topic ideation based on your industry and goals
- First 4 posts free — no risk
