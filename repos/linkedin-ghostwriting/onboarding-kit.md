# LinkedIn Ghostwriting — Onboarding Kit

> Internal process document. This is how you handle a client from first enquiry to ongoing monthly delivery.

---

## When Someone Enquires

### Step 1: First Response (within 24 hours)

Reply via email or LinkedIn DM:

```
Hey [name],

Thanks for reaching out about the LinkedIn ghostwriting! 

Quick question before we set up a call — what do you do, and what's one topic you'd post about on LinkedIn if you had the time? Just helps me prep before we chat.

Talk soon,
Zephr
```

### Step 2: Book the Discovery Call

- Use Google Meet (free) — no need for Calendly or paid scheduling tools
- 45 minutes is the sweet spot
- Send a calendar invite if they're keen, or just pick a time in DMs

### Step 3: The Discovery Call (45 min)

**Record the call** (with permission — use Google Meet recording, free).

**Questions to ask (in order):**

1. "Tell me about your business — what do you do, who do you serve?"
2. "Who do you want reading your LinkedIn posts? Clients, investors, talent, peers?"
3. "What's something you believe that most people in your industry would disagree with?"
4. "What frustrates you about your industry?"
5. "What are you proud of that you never get to talk about publicly?"
6. "What's your tone — are you casual, direct, sarcastic, earnest, academic?"
7. "Are there any topics that are off-limits? Anything you don't want to touch?"
8. "Who do you admire on LinkedIn? What do you like about how they post?"
9. "What does success look like for you in 3 months of posting?"
10. "How involved do you want to be — do you want to approve every post, or do you trust me to write and you'll just post?"

**What to listen for:**
- Their actual phrasing and vocabulary (write these down — you'll use them)
- Their opinions (these become post topics)
- Their energy (casual vs formal, funny vs serious)
- Their stories (anecdotes make the best posts)

### Step 4: Write the First 4 Posts

**Format:** Google Doc, shared with the client.

**Each post structure:**
- Hook (first line that stops the scroll) — 1 sentence
- Body (the actual point) — 3-6 short paragraphs
- Close (a question, a challenge, or a contrarian note) — 1-2 sentences
- Total: 150-300 words

**Voice rules:**
- Use their actual phrases from the call
- Use their opinions — don't soften them
- Match their tone (if they're blunt, be blunt; if they're funny, be funny)
- No corporate jargon they wouldn't use
- Read it out loud — if it doesn't sound like them, rewrite it

**Send the doc with this note:**

```
Hey [name],

Here are your first 4 posts. Read them out loud — if they don't sound like you, tell me what's off and I'll fix them. Don't worry about being nice, I want them to be RIGHT.

If they sound like you, post the first one this week. I'll watch the engagement and adjust the next batch based on what's working.

Posts:
1. [Post 1 — lead with their strongest opinion]
2. [Post 2 — a story or anecdote from the call]
3. [Post 3 — a contrarian take on their industry]
4. [Post 4 — something personal / behind the scenes]

Talk soon,
Zephr
```

### Step 5: After They Post

- Watch engagement (likes, comments, shares) for each post
- Note which topics/format got the most response
- After all 4 are posted, send:

```
Hey [name],

All 4 posts are up. Here's what I noticed:

- Post [X] got the most engagement — [why I think it worked]
- Post [Y] didn't get much traction — [why, and what I'd change]

If you're happy with how this went, I'd love to keep going. 8 posts/month for $[price]. Same process — I write, you approve, you post.

Let me know what you think.

Zephr
```

### Step 6: Ongoing Monthly Process

**Week 1 of each month:**
- Write 8 posts in a shared Google Doc
- Send to client for review

**Week 2:**
- Client reviews, tweaks, approves
- Client posts 2/week (or schedule them via Buffer free tier)

**Throughout the month:**
- Track engagement on each post
- Note what's working for next month's batch
- Screenshot good engagement for case studies (with permission)

**End of month:**
- Send a brief summary: "Here's what performed best this month, here's what I'm adjusting for next month"
- Ask for payment (bank transfer, PayPal, or whatever they prefer)

## Getting Your First Client

Post this on LinkedIn:

```
I'm looking to ghostwrite LinkedIn posts for 3 founders — for free.

Here's the deal:
→ I write 4 posts in your voice
→ You review, you approve, you post
→ If you like them, we can talk about ongoing work
→ If you don't like them, you walk away, no cost

I write short, punchy, human posts — no corporate jargon. I've been building my own LinkedIn and Substack presence for months. Now I want to do it for someone else.

DM me if you're in. First 3 only.
```

Post it. Share it. Wait. The "free" and "first 3 only" combo creates urgency.

## FAQ for Clients

**"How do you write in my voice?"**
I do a 45-minute call where I ask you questions and listen to how you talk. I note your phrases, your opinions, your tone. Then I write using your vocabulary and perspective — not mine.

**"What if I don't like the posts?"**
The first 4 posts are free. If you don't like them, you walk away. No cost, no obligation. If you like them but want changes, tell me what's off and I'll fix them.

**"Do I have to post them myself?"**
Yes — you review, you approve, you post. This keeps your account authentic and you in control. I can help you schedule via Buffer (free) if you want to batch posts.

**"How long are the posts?"**
150-300 words. Short enough to read in 30 seconds, long enough to say something worth reading.

**"Can I edit the posts before posting?"**
Absolutely. They're your posts. I write them, you own them. Tweak, edit, rewrite — whatever you need.

**"What if I want to stop?"**
No lock-in. Cancel anytime. You just don't renew for the next month.
