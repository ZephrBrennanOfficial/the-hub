## 📌 About This Repository

Welcome to my **Sales Practice Lab**.

I'm Zephr Brennan — building a career in sales from scratch. I don't have years of quota-carrying experience, and I'm not going to pretend I do. What I have is relentless curiosity, discipline, and a commitment to mastering the fundamentals of modern B2B sales.

Instead of just saying I'm "passionate about sales," I built this repository to prove it. Here you'll find my mock discovery transcripts, cold email experiments, book notes, objection roleplay logs, and a 6-month learning roadmap.

Every script includes a self-critique — what I got right, what I missed, and what a senior rep would do differently. This repo is intentionally candid.

## 💡 My Learning Philosophy

1. **Deliberate practice over passive reading.** Every book chapter gets turned into a script, email, or roleplay.
2. **Brutal self-awareness.** I audit my own mock calls for filler words, rushed transitions, and missed cues.
3. **Psychology first.** Cold outreach isn't about pitching — it's about understanding prospect pain and decision-making.
4. **Consistency over intensity.** Daily reps beat occasional bursts.
