## 🗺️ Sales Process Architecture Overview

This roadmap defines the 10 sequential stages of a modern, buyer-centric B2B revenue pipeline. Every stage includes explicit **Gate Exit Criteria** to ensure pipeline cleanliness and predictable forecasting.

## 🔬 Detailed Stage Breakdown

---

### Stage 1: Identify (Targeting & Lead Intelligence)

- **Stage Goal:** Identify high-probability target accounts and key buyer decision-makers matching the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- **Key Activities:**
  - Map target accounts using firmographic filters (revenue, headcount, geography, technology stack).
  - Identify key personas (Economic Buyer, Champion, Technical Evaluator, End User).
  - Verify email addresses and direct dial contact numbers.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Account verified against ICP criteria; at least 3 buying committee contacts mapped with verified contact data.
- **Common Mistakes:** Building massive unverified lists; targeting single contacts rather than multi-threading the account.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** ZoomInfo, Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, BuiltWith, Clay.

---

### Stage 2: Connect (Outreach & Initial Touch)

- **Stage Goal:** Break through noise to start meaningful 2-way conversations with key decision-makers.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Deploy multi-touch, multi-channel sequences (Email, LinkedIn, Phone, Direct Mail) over 14-21 days.
  - Deliver value-first content, industry insights, or benchmark reports.
  - Handle initial response objections and secure discovery call bookings.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Target prospect replies and agrees to a scheduled 30-minute discovery call.
- **Common Mistakes:** Pitch-slapping in message #1; relying solely on cold email without phone/LinkedIn touchpoints.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Outreach.io, Salesloft, Lemlist, Gong, PhantomBuster.

---

### Stage 3: Qualify (Initial Triage & SLA Handoff)

- **Stage Goal:** Filter out non-fit leads quickly to protect sales team bandwidth and ensure high pipeline quality.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Execute initial screening call (SDR) or pre-discovery review.
  - Evaluate basic framework criteria (Need, Authority, Budget, Timeline alignment).
  - Complete SDR to AE lead handover brief.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Sales Qualified Opportunity (SQO) accepted by AE; meeting held and confirmed against SLA.
- **Common Mistakes:** Passing unqualified leads to AEs to inflate pipeline metrics; hoarding dead leads.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, Chili Piper, Calendly.

---

### Stage 4: Discover (Diagnostic & Pain Quantification)

- **Stage Goal:** Uncover underlying operational pain points, quantify financial cost of inaction, and establish desired future state.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Execute structured discovery framework using open-ended diagnostic questions.
  - Quantify financial and operational impact of current friction points.
  - Map decision-making hierarchy (MEDDPICC) and identify internal Champion.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Quantified business pain validated by prospect; Economic Buyer identified; mutual agreement to attend tailored solution presentation.
- **Common Mistakes:** Rushing into product demos before quantifying pain; failing to identify the Economic Buyer early.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Gong.io, Chorus.ai, Notion/Google Docs (Discovery Templates).

---

### Stage 5: Present (Tailored Solution Demo)

- **Stage Goal:** Present a custom solution mapped directly to the pains, metrics, and goals uncovered during Discovery.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Deliver a tailored demonstration or strategy presentation focused on business outcomes.
  - Highlight relevant peer case studies and quantifiable ROI proof points.
  - Conduct interactive check-ins to confirm solution alignment.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Prospect confirms proposed solution addresses core pain points; agreement to review formal business case and proposal.
- **Common Mistakes:** Giving generic "feature tours"; failing to link product capabilities back to quantified discovery pains.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Zoom, Demostack, Reprise, Google Slides / Canva.

---

### Stage 6: Propose (Business Case & Mutual Action Plan)

- **Stage Goal:** Co-create a formal business case and backward-mapped timeline for deployment.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Deliver customized proposal with clear pricing tiers, scope of work, and ROI calculation.
  - Co-author a Mutual Action Plan (MAP) mapping required steps through go-live.
  - Review proposal directly with Economic Buyer and key stakeholders.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Mutual Action Plan approved by Economic Buyer; pricing and scope verbally agreed upon.
- **Common Mistakes:** Emailing proposals without a live review meeting; omitting a Mutual Action Plan.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** PandaDoc, DocuSign, Qwilr, ROI Calculator Tools.

---

### Stage 7: Negotiate (Risk Mitigation & Commercial Alignment)

- **Stage Goal:** Resolve final commercial, legal, and operational objections while preserving deal margin and value.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Execute AAAR objection framework for price or timing hesitations.
  - Trade concessions value-for-value (e.g., offer price discount only in exchange for multi-year commitment or upfront payment).
  - Navigate preliminary legal, security, and procurement reviews.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Final contract terms, pricing, and scope approved by legal and procurement teams.
- **Common Mistakes:** Discounting prematurely without receiving commercial concessions in return; ignoring procurement lead times.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Ironclad, RevOps, Concord.

---

### Stage 8: Close (Contract Execution & Final Sign-off)

- **Stage Goal:** Secure formal contract execution and initiate internal account transition.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Issue final executable contract via e-signature platform.
  - Confirm receipt of signed Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Statement of Work (SOW).
  - Conduct internal sales-to-onboarding debrief.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Fully executed contract received; initial deposit or purchase order processed; deal marked Closed-Won in CRM.
- **Common Mistakes:** Celebrating before the signature is executed; failing to prepare customer success for handover.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** DocuSign, Adobe Sign, Salesforce, Slack (Deal Alerts).

---

### Stage 9: Onboard (Implementation & First Value Realization)

- **Stage Goal:** Ensure smooth customer onboarding and rapid realization of initial value.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Conduct formal Customer Success Handoff Call with client and AE present.
  - Kick off implementation timeline according to Mutual Action Plan.
  - Track Time-to-First-Value (TTFV) metric.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Core platform deployment complete; primary users trained; first key value milestone achieved within 30-60 days.
- **Common Mistakes:** AE completely disappearing post-sale; disconnect between sales promises and implementation reality.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Gainsight, ChurnZero, Asana, Monday.com.

---

### Stage 10: Retain (Value Verification, Upsell & Expansion)

- **Stage Goal:** Secure account renewal, expand account lifetime value (LTV), and generate advocate referrals.
- **Key Activities:**
  - Conduct Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) measuring ROI against discovery baselines.
  - Identify expansion triggers (new departments, increased user seat count, module add-ons).
  - Request customer testimonials, case studies, or warm network referrals.
- **Success Metrics & Exit Criteria:**
  - **Exit Gate:** Annual contract renewed; upsell/expansion opportunity identified and logged; customer reference secured.
- **Common Mistakes:** Waiting until 30 days before renewal to engage customer; failing to measure ongoing ROI.
- **Recommended Tech Stack:** Gainsight, Totango, SurveyMonkey (NPS), LinkedIn (Referral Mapping).
