# First Discovery Call Playbook & Execution Template

**Author:** Zephr Brennan | Sales Consulting Toolkit  
**Target Role:** Account Executives, Business Development Managers, Sales Reps  
**Duration:** 30–45 Minutes | **Format:** Video Call / Phone  

---

## 📋 Phase 1: Pre-Call Research Checklist (10 Minutes Prior)

Do **NOT** jump on a discovery call without completing this 6-point pre-flight check.

- [ ] **Company Basics:** Verify company size, industry, revenue estimate, and recent funding/news via LinkedIn / Crunchbase / Company Website.
- [ ] **Prospect Profile:** Check job title, tenure in role, previous employment history, and recent posts/articles on LinkedIn.
- [ ] **Tech Stack Audit:** Check tech stack tools using BuiltWith or Wappalyzer (identify CRM, marketing automation, existing software).
- [ ] **Hiring Signals:** Search company job boards for open positions (indicates growth areas, pain points, or staffing shortages).
- [ ] **CRM / History Check:** Review previous interactions, past closed-lost notes, or colleagues who connected with this account.
- [ ] **Hypothesis of Pain:** Formulate 2 plausible hypotheses based on their role and industry before asking a single question.

## 🎙️ Phase 2: Call Opening & Agenda Setting (Minutes 0 – 5)

### 1. Warm Greeting & Time Check
> *"Hi [Prospect First Name], great to connect today. First off, I want to respect your time—we scheduled 30 minutes today. Does that still work for you?"*

### 2. Upfront Contract & Agenda Setting
> *"Perfect. The goal of today's call isn't to give you a polished pitch or push a product. My objective is simple: to learn a bit about how [Company Name] is currently managing [Core Focus Area, e.g., sales process & pipeline conversion], understand where you're aiming to go this year, and see if there’s a fit for us to help.*
>
> *Here’s how I suggest we structure our 30 minutes:*
> 1. **First 15-20 mins:** I’ll ask a few targeted questions about your current setup, priorities, and any bottlenecks.
> 2. **Next 5-10 mins:** If it sounds like we can help, I’ll share a quick high-level overview of how we solve those specific issues.
> 3. **Final 5 mins:** We’ll decide together whether it makes sense to schedule a tailored deep-dive or part ways.
>
> *How does that agenda sound to you?"*

### 3. Permission to Take Notes
> *"I’ll be taking a few notes as we speak to ensure I capture your exact feedback. Do you mind if I jot down notes while we talk?"*

## ❓ Phase 3: Core Discovery Question Bank (Minutes 5 – 25)

*Rule of Thumb: Ask open-ended questions (Who, What, How, Tell me about...). Do NOT stack questions.*

---

### Theme A: Current Situation & Baseline Architecture
*Goal: Map their existing workflow and environment.*

1. *"To start, could you walk me through how your team currently handles [Process, e.g., lead triage from inbound contact to first AE demo]?"*
2. *"What tools or platforms is your team currently using to manage this today?"*
3. *"How many reps/team members are actively operating in this workflow?"*
4. *"When you look at your team's current performance metrics, what stands out as working really well?"*

---

### Theme B: Pain Points & Cost of Status Quo
*Goal: Uncover friction, quantify impact, and stir latent dissatisfaction.*

1. *"Where do you see the biggest friction or operational delay in that process today?"*
2. *"When deals stall or prospects drop out of your funnel, where is that happening most frequently?"*
3. *"If you had to estimate, how much time/revenue do you think the team is losing every month due to [uncovered pain]?"*
4. *"How is this issue impacting you personally in your role right now?"*
5. *"What happens if you do nothing and leave this process as it is for the next 6 to 12 months?"*

---

### Theme C: Future State & Goals
*Goal: Define specific, measurable business outcomes.*

1. *"Looking ahead over the next 2 to 3 quarters, what are the top 2-3 revenue or growth targets you're held accountable for?"*
2. *"If we were having this conversation 12 months from now and celebrating a wild success, what specific numbers would we be looking at?"*
3. *"What key capabilities would your team need in place to make that goal realistic?"*

---

### Theme D: Decision Process & Stakeholder Mapping
*Goal: Understand how decisions get made and who holds power.*

1. *"When [Company Name] has evaluated and purchased tools/consulting solutions like this in the past, what did that decision process look like?"*
2. *"Besides yourself, who else in the executive team (e.g., CFO, VP Sales, Ops, CEO) will need to sign off on a project like this?"*
3. *"What key criteria will your team use to evaluate whether a solution is worth investing in?"*
4. *"What potential hurdles or internal security/procurement steps usually slow down vendor onboarding at your company?"*

---

### Theme E: Budget, Value & Timeline
*Goal: Determine financial viability and urgency.*

1. *"Is there an active budget already allocated for fixing [pain point] this fiscal year, or would this require securing new funding?"*
2. *"What kind of financial return or payback timeline would your executive team expect to justify an investment like this?"*
3. *"Is there a specific hard date or compelling event (e.g., quarterly kickoff, board meeting, new hire cohort) driving the timing for this?"*

## 🎯 Phase 4: Modern Qualification Framework (MEDDPICC Adapted)

Use this quick scorecard right after the discovery call to evaluate deal health:

| Qualification Gate | Key Diagnostic Question | Status (Green / Amber / Red) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **M - Metrics** | Has the buyer agreed on quantifiable metrics (e.g., +20% conversion, -$50k waste)? | `[ ]` | |
| **E - Economic Buyer** | Have we identified and made contact with the person who holds budget approval? | `[ ]` | |
| **D - Decision Criteria** | Do we know their exact technical, commercial, and operational evaluation criteria? | `[ ]` | |
| **D - Decision Process** | Is the step-by-step approval and procurement roadmap clear? | `[ ]` | |
| **P - Paper Process** | Do we know their legal, NDA, security review, and contract timelines? | `[ ]` | |
| **I - Identified Pain** | Is there a clear, acute business pain with severe consequences if unsolved? | `[ ]` | |
| **C - Champion** | Do we have an internal ally with power and influence fighting for our solution? | `[ ]` | |
| **C - Competition** | Do we know what competing alternatives (including doing nothing) are being evaluated? | `[ ]` | |

## 🤝 Phase 5: Closing the Call & Securing Next Steps (Minutes 25 – 30)

### 1. Summarize Pain & Validate
> *"We’ve covered a lot today, [Prospect First Name]. To make sure I’ve got this right: your main priority is [Primary Goal], but right now [Pain Point 1] and [Pain Point 2] are slowing the team down and costing roughly [Quantified Impact]. Did I miss anything critical?"*

### 2. Transition to Solution Preview (2 Minutes)
> *"Based on what you've shared, this is very aligned with work we’ve done with companies like [Similar Client Reference]. Specifically, we helped them solve [Pain Point] by implementing [Brief Core Framework], resulting in [Key Metric Result, e.g., a 28% increase in closed-won conversion]."*

### 3. Propose Specific Next Step with Agenda
> *"Rather than diving into a generic demo today, I’d like to prepare a customized presentation tailored directly to your team's workflow and build a initial business case for your CFO.*
>
> *How does your calendar look next [Tuesday/Wednesday] at [10:00 AM]? On that call, we’ll bring in [Key Stakeholder title if needed], review the tailored strategy, and review concrete ROI figures."*
