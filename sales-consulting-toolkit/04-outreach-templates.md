## 📧 Section 1: Cold Email Templates

### Template 1.1: The Executive Value Proposition (Outbound to C-Suite / VP)

#### 🎯 When to Use
Use when initiating contact with senior decision-makers (VP Sales, Chief Revenue Officer, Founder) at mid-market or enterprise companies. Best deployed when you have identified a clear operational trigger (e.g., rapid hiring, missed target public statements, expansion into new markets).

#### ✉️ Email Template

```text
Subject: [First Name] - quick question re: [Pain Point / Growth Goal] at [Company]

Hi [First Name],

Noticed [Company] recently [insert trigger, e.g., expanded the sales team by 12 reps / launched into the APAC market]. Congrats on the momentum.

When revenue teams scale this quickly, we usually see [Primary Bottleneck, e.g., discovery call conversion drop by 15-20% because new reps lack a standardized playbook].

We recently helped [Similar Company Name] solve this exact gap—increasing their AE win rate from [Baseline %, e.g., 18%] to [New %, e.g., 27%] within 60 days by [Brief Core Fix, e.g., auditing their discovery framework and embedding a quick-reference objection matrix].

Worth a 5-minute conversation to see if this is on your roadmap for [Current Quarter]? 

If not, no worries at all.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Title] | Zephr Brennan Consulting
[LinkedIn Profile Link]
```

#### 🧠 Why It Works
1. **Relevance over Personalization:** Instead of superficial praise ("I saw you went to Auckland Uni"), it highlights a specific business trigger relevant to their job.
2. **Quantified Peer Social Proof:** Mentions a direct peer/competitor with precise before-and-after conversion metrics.
3. **Low-Friction Call-to-Action (CTA):** Asks for permission to chat ("Worth a 5-minute conversation?") rather than demanding a 30-minute calendar block.
4. **The "Opt-Out" Release:** Adding *"If not, no worries at all"* lowers buyer defensiveness and increases response rates by up to 22%.

#### 🛠️ Customization Notes
- Keep total word count under 120 words.
- Replace `[Similar Company Name]` with a recognizable company in their exact industry vertical.

### Template 1.2: The "Bump with Insight" (Follow-Up After No Response)

#### 🎯 When to Use
Send 3 to 4 business days after Email #1 has gone unanswered. Never use passive-aggressive bumps like "Just checking in on this" or "Did you see my last email?". Always bring fresh micro-value.

#### ✉️ Email Template

```text
Subject: Re: [First Name] - quick question re: [Pain Point / Growth Goal] at [Company]

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note from Tuesday with a quick 1-page resource.

I put together a brief checklist mapping the 7 places mid-market sales pipelines leak revenue during discovery calls: [Link to Resource / Audit Guide]. 

Page 2 specifically highlights how reps accidentally surrender price power during early objection handling.

Are you open to reviewing how [Company]'s current discovery conversion compares to industry benchmarks?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

#### 🧠 Why It Works
1. **Zero Guilt:** Does not chastise the reader for ignoring the first message.
2. **Adds Real Value:** Provides a standalone asset (audit checklist or benchmark guide) that educates before asking for anything in return.
3. **Short & Scannable:** Takes less than 15 seconds to read on a mobile screen.

#### 🛠️ Customization Notes
- Ensure the resource link goes to a clean, non-gated asset (PDF, Loom video, or notion document) so there is zero friction.

### Template 1.3: The Referral / Mutual Network Warm Intro

#### 🎯 When to Use
Use when you share a mutual contact, investor, advisor, or customer who gave you permission (or suggested) you reach out.

#### ✉️ Email Template

```text
Subject: [Mutual Contact Name] suggested I reach out to [First Name]

Hi [First Name],

I was speaking with [Mutual Contact Name] earlier this week about [Topic / Shared Interest], and your name came up as someone driving great work at [Company].

[Mutual Contact Name] mentioned you might be exploring ways to [Specific Strategic Goal, e.g., standardize AE discovery calls as you scale].

We recently helped [Peer Company] tackle that exact challenge, resulting in [Key Result, e.g., a 3.2x ROI on pipeline conversion within 90 days].

Would you be open to a brief 10-minute intro call next [Day of week, e.g., Thursday] morning?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

#### 🧠 Why It Works
1. **Borrowed Trust:** Placing the mutual contact's name in the Subject Line guarantees an 80%+ open rate.
2. **Immediate Context:** Explains the origin of the message within the first sentence.
3. **Specific Request:** Proposes a short 10-minute intro window.

## 🔗 Section 2: LinkedIn Connection Request Templates

### Template 2.1: The Low-Friction Value Connect (Under 300 Characters)

#### 🎯 When to Use
Default connection request for targeting ideal prospect buyer profiles on LinkedIn.

#### 💬 Template Copy (238 Characters)

> *"Hi [First Name] - loved your recent post on [Topic / Sales Strategy]. I regularly share practical frameworks for B2B pipeline conversion and discovery optimization for NZ/APAC tech leaders. Would love to connect here on LinkedIn!"*

#### 🧠 Why It Works
- Sub 300 characters (fits LinkedIn's strict note limit).
- Non-pitchy; sets the expectation of peer-to-peer connection rather than immediate pitch slap.

---

### Template 2.2: The Event / Content Trigger Connect

#### 🎯 When to Use
Use when a prospect attends an industry webinar, comments on a viral post, or speaks at a conference.

#### 💬 Template Copy (265 Characters)

> *"Hi [First Name] - saw you were attending [Event / Webinar Name, e.g., SaaS Growth Summit]. Really enjoyed [Speaker/Topic Name]'s session on rep productivity. Thought I'd connect with fellow B2B sales leaders following the event. Cheers, Zephr"*

#### 🧠 Why It Works
- Shares a common context (event attendance).
- Establishes instant peer status without sell-side pressure.

## 📥 Section 3: LinkedIn InMail Template

### Template 3.1: The Problem-Solution Mini-Pitch (InMail)

#### 🎯 When to Use
Use when messaging prospects outside your 1st-degree network via LinkedIn InMail credits.

#### 💬 InMail Template

```text
Subject: Benchmarking [Company]'s discovery conversion

Hi [First Name],

Noticed you’re leading the sales team at [Company]. Given your focus on [Growth Objective], I thought this might be relevant.

We've been auditing B2B sales pipelines across APAC and noticed a recurring pattern: over 60% of qualified deals stall in the "Discovery to Demo" stage because reps pitch features instead of quantifying customer pain.

We built a 1-page Discovery Call Framework that addresses this directly (helped [Client Name] bump win rates by 24%).

Would you be open if I dropped the PDF link over LinkedIn message? No call required.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

#### 🧠 Why It Works
- **"No Call Required" Hook:** Asking permission to send a PDF over message requires zero calendar commitment from the executive. Once they reply "Sure", you have a 1st-degree chat channel opened permanently!
- **Data-Driven Insight:** Cites a specific industry pattern that resonates with revenue leaders.
