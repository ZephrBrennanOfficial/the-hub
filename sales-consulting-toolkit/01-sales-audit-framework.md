**Author:** Zephr Brennan | Sales Consulting Toolkit  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Target Audience:** B2B Sales Leaders, Founders, Revenue Operations, Sales Consultants  

---

## 📌 Executive Overview & Methodology

This framework provides a structured, quantitative audit tool for evaluating an organization's end-to-end sales engine. By diagnosing performance across **7 core stages** with **35 key criteria**, consultants and sales leaders can pinpoint systemic bottlenecks, quantify operational maturity, and establish a prioritized 90-day remediation roadmap.

### Scoring Scale (1 to 5)

Each question is evaluated on a 1-to-5 maturity scale:

| Score | Rating | Maturity Level Definition |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Ad-Hoc / Absent** | Process is non-existent, chaotic, or dependent entirely on individual rep heroics. No documented standards. |
| **2** | **Informal** | Basic awareness exists, but execution is inconsistent across reps and undocumented. |
| **3** | **Defined** | Documented standard operates across the team, but enforcement and tracking are variable. |
| **4** | **Managed** | Process is standardized, regularly tracked in CRM, and reinforced through management coaching. |
| **5** | **Optimized** | Process is automated, continuously measured against benchmarks, and optimized with data-driven iteration. |

### Score Band Interpretation

#### 🔴 Score: 35 – 71 (0% – 39%) | *Reactive / Vulnerable State*
- **Diagnostic:** Sales performance relies heavily on random market luck or 1-2 star performers. The business lacks a repeatable system. High rep turnover, long deal cycles, and severe discounting are typical.
- **Immediate Priority:** Focus on Stage 2 (Qualification) and Stage 3 (Discovery). Stop wasting sales time on bad-fit prospects. Document a basic 4-stage pipeline.

#### 🟡 Score: 72 – 125 (40% – 69%) | *Emerging / Inconsistent State*
- **Diagnostic:** Processes are defined on paper but executed inconsistently. Reps operate in silos using personalized, unmeasured tactics. Outbound pipeline is unpredictable, and deals stall frequently in demo/proposal stages.
- **Immediate Priority:** Standardize Stage 1 (Outreach sequences), implement a mandatory Discovery Template (Stage 3), and train on structured Objection Handling (Stage 5).

#### 🟢 Score: 126 – 157 (70% – 87%) | *Structured / Growth State*
- **Diagnostic:** Strong operational foundation. CRM compliance is good, sales metrics are tracked, and winning behaviors are understood. Bottlenecks occur in enterprise deal navigation and post-sale expansion.
- **Immediate Priority:** Introduce Mutual Action Plans (Stage 6), formalize ROI business cases (Stage 4), and build automated re-engagement sequences (Stage 7).

#### 🔵 Score: 158 – 180 (88% – 100%) | *Optimized / High-Velocity Machine*
- **Diagnostic:** World-class revenue operation. Clear metrics across every stage, continuous A/B testing of messaging, frictionless sales-to-CS handoffs, and predictable pipeline generation.
- **Immediate Priority:** Focus on micro-optimizations: reduce sales cycle length by 10-15%, expand account retention rates, and integrate AI-assisted call analytics.

## 🛠️ Prioritized 90-Day Remediation Roadmap Template

When delivering this audit report to a client, use the following matrix to prioritize low-hanging fruit vs. strategic initiatives:

```
                  HIGH IMPACT
                      │
   [Quick Wins]       │     [Strategic Initiatives]
   • Standardize      │     • Redesign Outbound Sequences
     Discovery Guide  │     • Implement Mutual Action Plans
   • Build Objection  │     • Overhaul CRM Stage Criteria
     Matrix           │
                      │
 ─────────────────────┼───────────────────────────── LOW EFFORT
 HIGH EFFORT          │
   [Secondary Fixes]  │     [Low Priority]
   • Revamp Slide     │     • Complex Automation Rules
     Deck Styling     │     • Niche Referral Competitions
                      │
                      │
                  LOW IMPACT
```

### Action Plan Framework:
1. **Days 1–30 (Foundation):** Fix Qualification & Discovery templates. Train reps on business pain identification.
2. **Days 31–60 (Enablement):** Roll out objection handling playbooks, competitive battlecards, and ROI calculators.
3. **Days 61–90 (Scaling & Optimization):** Establish post-sale handoff protocols, mutual action plans, and automated CRM tracking.
