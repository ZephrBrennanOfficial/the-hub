## 🗓️ Phase-by-Phase Execution Plan

### 🚀 Phase 1: Foundations, Discovery & Qualification (Months 1 - 2)
**Focus:** Developing active listening skills, structured questioning methodologies, and basic CRM mechanics.

#### Key Milestones & Deliverables
* Read and create actionable study notes for *SPIN Selling* and *Never Split the Difference*.
* Record and self-critique **15 mock discovery calls** simulating B2B SaaS and agency scenarios.
* Obtain official certification: **HubSpot Inbound Sales Certification**.
* Build a personal "Questioning Framework Matrix" (Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff).

#### Weekly Time Allocation
* **10 hours:** Reading & methodology study.
* **8 hours:** Mock call drills and recording analysis.
* **5 hours:** Certification course material.
* **2 hours:** Reviewing call recordings from Gong Public Library.

---

### 📞 Phase 2: Outbound Prospecting & Cold Calling Mechanics (Months 3 - 4)
**Focus:** Copywriting, overcoming call anxiety, cold call cadence structure, and objection handling under pressure.

#### Key Milestones & Deliverables
* Read *Fanatical Prospecting* and *New Sales. Simplified.*
* Draft and A/B test **50 cold email templates** across 5 distinct target personas.
* Execute **200+ simulated cold calls** using cold call script frameworks (Anchor-Reason-Ask).
* Complete **40 objection handling drills** covering price, timing, competitor, and brush-off objections.
* Master tech stack fundamentals: Hands-on navigation of **HubSpot CRM**, **Apollo.io**, **Salesloft**, and **Salesforce Trailhead**.

#### Weekly Time Allocation
* **10 hours:** Cold outreach copywriting and cold call drills.
* **8 hours:** Objection roleplay with study partners/mentors.
* **5 hours:** Tech stack tutorials & Salesforce Trailhead modules.
* **2 hours:** Listening to cold call breakdowns on YouTube & podcasts.

---

### 📈 Phase 3: Business Acumen & Executive Communication (Month 5)
**Focus:** Understanding B2B financial metrics, reading annual reports, and speaking the language of executive decision-makers.

#### Key Milestones & Deliverables
* Read *The Challenger Sale*.
* Learn core SaaS financial unit economics: **ARR, MRR, CAC, LTV, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), CAC Payback Period**.
* Practice reading 3 public SaaS company **10-K annual reports** (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake) to identify corporate strategic initiatives and risk factors.
* Map out Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and Buyer Personas for 3 different tech verticals (DevOps, HR Tech, EdTech).

#### Weekly Time Allocation
* **10 hours:** SaaS business model study and financial analysis.
* **8 hours:** Executive persona mapping and value proposition drafting.
* **7 hours:** Reading industry newsletters and tech market reports.

---

### 🎯 Phase 4: Portfolio Finalization & Prospecting Hiring Managers (Month 6)
**Focus:** Applying outbound SDR tactics directly to the job hunt—treating hiring managers as prospective clients.

#### Key Milestones & Deliverables
* Finalize all documentation in this **GitHub Sales Practice Lab**.
* Conduct 10 live mock interview roleplays with active SDR Managers and Account Executives for feedback.
* Execute an outbound campaign targeting Sales Directors and VP of Sales at target companies, using personalized video and custom pitches (practicing what I preach).
* Secure and execute interviews for entry-level SDR/BDR roles.
