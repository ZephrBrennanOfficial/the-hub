About

The person behind the work

I'm Zephr — an early-career professional based in Auckland, Aotearoa New Zealand. My background spans customer service, telefundraising, and freelance work in theatre and makeup artistry. None of that is a traditional path into business strategy or tech.

But I've learned that starting from the bottom is a perspective you can't fake. When you've been the person making the cold calls, fielding the objections, and hearing the rejections — you understand things about sales and human behaviour that no course can teach.

I write on Substack, build things publicly on GitHub, and run Quill — a LinkedIn ghostwriting service for founders. The goal isn't to skip steps. It's to earn every single one of them.

"The view from here teaches you things the top never will."
